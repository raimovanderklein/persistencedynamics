## Brand canon (non-negotiable)

**Colours.**
- Potentiality (Pot): `#1B5BD8` (blue)
- Construction (C): `#B77800` (amber)
- Encounter (E): `#D63A1F` (red)
- Conservation (Co): `#924D35` (russet)
- Wordmark / body text: `#4D4843` (warm grey)
- Observer: `#2E5E3A` (green, only if introduced — not used in this article)
- Background: white

**Position of the four regimes (fixed across all surfaces).**
- Potentiality = top-left (10 o'clock)
- Construction = top-right
- Encounter = bottom-right
- Conservation = bottom-left
- Traversal is **clockwise**: Pot → C → E → Co → Pot

**Two crossed axes under the cycle.**
- Transactional axis (spatial, giving and taking): Pot ↔ Encounter, the top-left to bottom-right diagonal
- Generational axis (temporal, loss and birth): Construction ↔ Conservation, the top-right to bottom-left diagonal

**Typography.**
- Familjen Grotesk 700 for headings
- Inter Tight for body
- JetBrains Mono for any code or data labels
