# Palintropy — Core

The source of truth. Everything is built to this; nothing in code may contradict
it. Palintropy is a structure for organising work as change. It is not a list and
not a tree of tasks. It is one recursive cycle, addressed like a language, that
holds where every thing stands, what it is stuck on, and what must still close for
it to transform.

---

## 1. The cycle is the atom

Two operations: **hold** (gather, retain, the past) and **cross** (release, hand
forward, the future). Each is active or latent. Two operations with two states
give four configurations, and each is a **regime**:

| regime | hold·cross | type | operation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Potentiality | active·active | capacitor | **load** — gather to a threshold, release |
| Construction | latent·active | factory | **form** — select one form, assemble it |
| Encounter | latent·latent | reactor | **meet** — the form contacts what it is for |
| Conservation | active·latent | homeostat | **secure** — keep what survived, release the rest |

The four run in one **forced order**: Potentiality, Construction, Encounter,
Conservation, and back. It is the unique 2-bit Gray code (exactly one operation
flips per step) and it is substrate-forced (each regime produces what the next
consumes). The order cannot be reordered or skipped. A round **closes** when
Conservation hands its residue back as the next Potentiality's material,
contracting by 1/φ so the recursion is scale-free.

This is the whole atom. Nothing below is a new mechanism; it is this, repeated.

**The two bits generate how a position is measured — one rule, derived, every
depth.** Do not author a rule per regime; derive it from `hold` and `cross`:

| | cross=1 — binary event (make-it-or-don't) | cross=0 — continuous result (magnitude, momentum) |
|---|---|---|
| hold=1 — charge / keep | Potentiality: a gated release → **no clock** (a judgment, can't be timed) | Conservation: a held setpoint → **review / drift** |
| hold=0 — release / expose | Construction: a finished form → **deadline** | Encounter: a world result → **results signal** |

- **Result kind = `cross`.** cross=1 is binary — a prototype is binary *by
  definition*: it makes it or it does not. cross=0 is continuous, given by the
  standing relation with the world. A binary result in a cross=0 position is a
  category error — the characteristic failure of that position (e.g. Engaging
  read as pass/fail is really a prototype, not an encounter).
- **Resolution = `cross`.** cross=1 resolves through a binary **child** (a
  prototype — this is gearing). cross=0 resolves by **acting** to grow or hold
  the result, never a pass/fail child.
- **Clock = the pair** — none / deadline / result / review, per the table.
- **Failure = a neighbour's logic applied here.**

Authored once in `cycle.semantics(hold, cross)`; `read`, `judgment`, and
`present` consume it. It holds at depth 16 and 64 by composition.

## 2. Two coordinates address everything

A thing carries two independent coordinates. Keep them apart.

**Internal coordinate — the address.** Where a thing is in its own cycle, written
as a path of phases. Depth 1 is the 4 realms, depth 2 the 16 challenges, depth 3
the 64 parts. The address *is* the position; there is no second numbering and no
lenses. Same rules at every depth (scale-invariant): a human reads it at 16, an
agent reads it at 64 and understands that position 9 is itself a small cycle with
a sub-position. The internal coordinate is **placed, not derived** from activity.

**Relational coordinate — the four cases.** How a thing connects to other things.
A thing relates through exactly four slots, the four poles of the two axes:

- transactional axis (space, giving and taking): **source** ↔ **object**
- generational axis (time, spending and carrying): **means** ↔ **end**

That is the entire, finite set of relations. There is no fifth kind of edge.

---

## 3. Identity is relational, and the relations are grammar

A thing is a cycle in itself, and it is also whatever role it plays in other
cycles. Its **four identities** are the four cases other things point at it
through: the same thing is the source of one cycle, the object of another, the
means of a third, the end of a fourth, all at once.

The four cases are not metaphors. They are the case system of a language. A cycle
is a **verb** (the act of change); its four slots are its grammar:

> **[source]** does → **[object]**, by **[means]**, toward **[end]**.

The two axes are the two deepest pairings every human language has: subject and
object, means and end. So a workspace is not a database of projects, it is a
**text in a change language**: nouns are verbs, the four cases are syntax, a
child is a subordinate clause, closure is the period that becomes a word in the
next sentence. Reference and meaning are one operation: to address a thing in
context is to name its case, e.g. `B.object`, `(B.object).means`. Hyperlinking is
what happens when the same thing fills a slot in more than one sentence.

## 4. Gearing: stuckness creates children

A thing runs its cycle, trying to close. At any position the work is either
resolvable at its own level or it is not.

- **Resolvable** → resolve it here (place it forward).
- **Not resolvable** → the thing is **stuck**, and it **spawns a child**: a whole
  new cycle whose only job is to resolve what the parent could not. The child is
  whatever that takes (a prototype, a decision, a hire, a study, a partnership).

The parent holds, stuck, while the child is open. The child's **resolution** is
what gears the parent:

- **Integrates** (the child closes successfully, its residue is kept) → the
  parent passes that position and the residue becomes the parent's substrate.
- **Fails** (the child closes dropped) → the parent is still stuck, and the
  position wants another child. Failure is expected and is information, not a
  reset.

Children are therefore **emergent**, not planned decomposition: the tree grows
exactly where the work jams. A workspace is the living record of where things
could not be resolved directly and what was created to get past them.

There is exactly one verb set, everywhere, at every depth: **resolve here, or
spawn a child.**

---

## 5. Closure: the full ordered set, or it did not happen

A cycle is **closed** only when every one of its positions has closed, **in the
fixed order**. Sixteen at the human zoom, sixty-four at the agent's. Skip one and
the cycle is not closed, it is stuck, however finished it looks from outside.
This is what change is: transformation is the complete ordered passage, never a
shortcut.

A child **integrates** upward only once it has closed its own full ordered set.
So "closed" is strict and rare. Most things sit mid-order: on their current
position, children open, the rest still owed. The system is, at root, a strict
per-thing accounting of which positions have closed, which is current, and what
is still owed, in order. That order is the whole value: it is what lets the
system show precisely what remains, and refuse to call a thing done when it
skipped.

---

## 6. Judgment is Palintropy's own compounding loop

Every read the engine emits, where a thing is, whether it needs a human, what the
move is, is a **falsifiable judgment** with a resolution condition. The thing's
own future resolves it: did the child integrate, did the human act, was the flag
right. The judgment is scored and **learns**, sharper on this world first, then as
priors across worlds. This loop is private and is Palintropy's. It is not Theory
(the public forecasting benchmark); they share only the method. The gearing event
in §4 and the judgment's resolution event are the **same event**: one closure
both moves the parent and scores the call.

Pressure is not only the clock. A **named blocker with nothing resolving it** is
stuck regardless of where its clock sits — the move is to clear it (spawn a
child), and that needs a human, so it floors the pressure and surfaces. Once a
child is handling it, the parent parks (the work is in the child). This is gearing
read as judgment: stuckness is a blocker without a child.

## 7. The boundary

The framework stays invisible. Internally everything carries its canon: regime
keys, the gloss names, the dynamical types. None of it crosses.

- **Agents** receive the **derived structure**: operation states, the act, what a
  position consumes and produces, how it fails, all generated by composition. No
  regime name, no gloss name, no type. This is the AI-native form, read at 64.
- **People** receive **plain language** at 16, plus, eventually, framing tuned to
  how they lead (parked; the seam exists).

A test scans every crossing for canon. If a name leaks, the build fails.

---

## 8. What the database must be

Not rows of projects. A **graph of cycles**:

- **nouns** — each a cycle, carrying its internal address.
- **cases** — directed edges typed by the four roles (source, object, means,
  end); the syntax.
- **children** — spawned on stuckness, bound to the parent position they resolve;
  closure events propagate upward.

A thing is simultaneously a cycle and an element in others. Recursion is native:
an edge's endpoints are themselves cycles. Postgres can hold this (a nouns table
plus a typed-edge table), but the shape is a graph and a grammar, not a list.

---

## 9. Laws (the invariants code must enforce)

1. The four regimes run in the forced order. Nothing reorders them.
2. A position cannot close before its predecessor; a cycle is not closed until all
   its positions have, in order.
3. At any position the move is to resolve it. *How* is derived from `cross`:
   cross=1 (binary) resolves through a child — a prototype that makes it or does
   not; cross=0 (continuous) resolves by acting to grow or hold the result. The
   resolution kind is not chosen.
4. A child integrates upward only on its own full, ordered closure.
5. Closure feeds up: a closed child's residue becomes its parent's substrate.
6. Position is placed (or advanced by a child integrating), never inferred from a
   noisy activity stream.
7. Same rules at every depth. Humans read 16, agents read 64.
8. Only plain language (to people) or derived structure (to agents) crosses the
   boundary. The canon never does.
9. How a position is measured — its result kind, clock, and resolution — is
   **derived from its two bits** (`cycle.semantics`), never authored per regime.
