§1

Five axioms

The seed. Everything else on this site is built from these, or is marked as an assumption.

A1

Hold and cross. A persistor has two operations at its boundary: hold, which keeps self, and cross, which engages other.

A2

Each on or off. Each operation is active or latent. A persistor has two bits.

A3

Recursive. Every persistor contains persistors and sits inside one. The same two bits appear at every scale.

A4

Mass conserved. Total mass across a closed system of persistors is constant.

A5

Motion exists. Cycles run, gradients drive flows, and crossing engages.

§2

The unit: the persistor

A persistor is a cycle that creates the structure for the next cycle to build on.

It is one moment in two dimensions, with four faces, and the moment iterates. Without closure there is no persistor: the Conservation of one round becomes the Potentiality of the next. The specification gives its axioms, its formal core, the recursion rules and how sure each one is.
