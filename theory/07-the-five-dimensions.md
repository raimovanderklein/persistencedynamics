# What has to be in place

Five conditions have to hold before the cycle can be seen running in a real system. Each rests on the one before it.

<!-- fig:five -->

1. **Identity.** There is something coherent enough to hold. A cell with a membrane, a team that knows who is in it.
2. **Other.** There is something beyond it to cross into. The cell has an environment, the team a market.
3. **Occurrence.** The engagement is actually happening, on real material, in real time.
4. **Closure.** A round completes, and what it produces becomes the material for the next round. The equation *d*² + *d* = 1 is this condition in formal terms.
5. **Recursion.** The closed cycle is one part of a larger cycle and contains smaller ones.

This gives a way to read any system. Find the lowest condition that is not in place and start there. A system that runs but does not close has occurrence without completion: work that never compounds. Work on any higher condition will not hold until the lower one does.
