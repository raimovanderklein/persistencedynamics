# The two crossed dimensions

The four regimes sit on two dimensions that cross at the centre of the cycle.

The **transactional dimension** runs from Potentiality to Encounter. It is the dimension of taking in and giving out. At Potentiality the system has taken in all it can hold; at Encounter it gives itself to the meeting it was built for. It spans space, because taking and giving happen between things in different places.

The **generational dimension** runs from Construction to Conservation. It is the dimension of loss and birth. Construction spends: it commits to one form at the cost of every other it could have built. Conservation carries forward: it keeps what worked and lets the rest go. It spans time, because spending and carrying forward happen between an earlier and a later state of the same system.

Every regime sits where one position on the transactional dimension meets one position on the generational dimension. This crossing of space and time is what the persistor is.
