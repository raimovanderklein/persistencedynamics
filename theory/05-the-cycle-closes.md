# The cycle closes

A round is complete when what leaves Conservation becomes the material for the next Potentiality. This is closure: the cycle gives back what it received, and also produces what the next round will run on. Without closure there is no persistence, only a single event.

Closure also sets the scale between levels. Let *d* be the contraction ratio per level. A cycle hands a fraction *d* of its content to the level above: first-order reach. That level hands on *d* of it in turn, *d*² of the original two levels up: second-order reach. For first-order and second-order reach to sum to one unit, *d* must satisfy

φ is not chosen. It is the only positive ratio at which the recursion closes, and it enters the theory at the level-crossing only. Every φ that appears later descends from this one equation.

The geometry does not change between a cell, a clinical trial, an organisation and a career. The substrate changes; the four regimes, the order and the closure condition stay the same. That is what makes the theory portable, and it is also where the theory would fail most clearly if it is wrong.
