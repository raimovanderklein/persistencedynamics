## How this lab uses it

Encounter applies Persistence Dynamics to immuno-oncology trial design. Cancer cells run a cycle. Immune cells run a cycle. The drug that is supposed to bridge them is itself a Construction output that has to land in an Encounter regime, and trial protocols can be read for which regime they assume is already loaded and which they treat as already operating. When the assumption is wrong, the readout of the trial is predictable in advance of the data.

The clinical work is where the framework either earns its place or does not. If Persistence Dynamics cannot operate at the level of trial design, where the stakes are highest and the data is most resistant to wishful reading, then it has no place. The articles on this site are written from inside that test, not from above it.
