# About

Persistence Dynamics is developed by **Raimo van der Klein**, founder of [Encounter](https://encounter.bio), a company in Hoofddorp, the Netherlands, that applies the theory to cancer. The theory grew out of that work: the question of why an immune response against a tumour holds or fails turned out to have the same shape as the question of why anything lasts.

## The paper

The theory was first published as a preprint in May 2026:

van der Klein, R. (2026). *Persistence Dynamics: The Internal Geometry of Dissipative-Autopoietic Systems.* Zenodo. [doi:10.5281/zenodo.20415561](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20415561)

The paper derives the two operations, the four regimes, the forced order, the closure condition and the recursion, and lists the measurements that would disprove them. The theory has developed since; a second version of the paper is in preparation.

## This site

This site covers the theory only: what it claims, how the claims follow from each other, and where it came from. Its application to cancer lives at [encounter.bio](https://encounter.bio), which is also the place to get in touch.
