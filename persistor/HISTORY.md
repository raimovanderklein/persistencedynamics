# Persistor: history

This page records the dated corrections, tests and renamings behind the [specification](spec.md), newest first. The working reference these come from is kept in [archive/canon-2026-05.md](archive/canon-2026-05.md).

| Date | Change | What it means for the spec |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-11-24 | The working canon was rewritten as specification v1.0. "Persistor" became the public name, with Change Unit and Quadragen as the same concept in other shapes. | This page and [spec.md](spec.md) were created |
| 2026-11-07 | A draft hypothesis proposed one closure product over eight relational factors, one per regime and one per threshold: Π = 1. | Not in the spec. It is still a hypothesis |
| 2026-05-27 | The five dimensions were reordered. Dimension 4 became closure, which had been ordering, and dimension 5 became recursion, which had been persistence. Ordering moved into Construction (R11), and persistence became the outcome of closure rather than a dimension. | §8.1 uses the new order |
| 2026-05-23 | The limits of the cohort tests were set out (bulk RNA, n = 232 NSCLC, one timepoint). The tests show the coordinate system is internally consistent on bulk observables. They do not validate the depth diagnostic, substrate time, or the gap reading of Potentiality. | R7, R9 and R11 are labelled "consistent on one cohort", never "validated" |
| 2026-05-22 | Potentiality was read as a structured gap left by the previous Conservation, and novelty as the correction-residue of failing cycles. | §4.1, §4.3 and §10 |
| 2026-05-20 | R13 was first tested on heart-rate variability (PhysioNet, n = 10). The predicted effect did not appear (p = 0.34), and the signal was weakly in the opposite direction. The bands tested were most likely sibling levels rather than parent and child, so the test pair was wrong. | R13 is labelled open |
| 2026-05-20 | The three speeds, R12, R13, the substrate economy and the failure-rate estimate were filed. | §9, §10 and §11 |
| 2026-05-14 | R5 was resolved: the crossing chooses the diameter. R7 held to 1.67 × 10⁻¹⁶ on the cohort, and R9 ran monotone by quartile (corr +0.189). | §7 |
| 2026-05-14 | The "three-circle vertex" strong form was tested. The 16 positions trace a ring, not the predicted 9 × 9 grid. | Falsified at bulk resolution. R1 to R11 do not depend on it |
| 2026-05-05 | The residence cascade 1 : φ : φ² : φ³ was falsified as an empirical universal. Cardiac phases measured 1 : 2.4 : 7.3 : 9.3 and the cell cycle 1 : 4 : 8 : 11, and no remapping rescues it. It survives only as a structural or normative form. The 9.47 envelope survives on two data points. The formula that converts one phase into a full cycle must not be used for prediction. | Listed as falsified in [claims.md](../claims.md) |
| 2026-05 | The Quadragen was renamed the Change Unit. | Both names are kept as shapes of the persistor |

## Left out of the spec

The working reference also holds material that the spec does not carry. It stays in the archive for now:

- Observer states and the perspective-operation gap, including the therapeutic order applied to people
- The "four shapes" of perspective and their composition into cultural forms
- A reading of simulation theory
- The mapping of the 16 positions onto personality archetypes
- Clinical intervention examples by regime and by verb
