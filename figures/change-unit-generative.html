The Encounter algorithm. Golid's Archetype procedure (2021): (1) partition a rectangle into sections; (2) divide each section into an equal-sized grid; (3) within each section, apply the same partition pattern to every cell. The repetition is what makes the chaos legible — random shapes alone read as noise; the same random shape tiled across a section reads as intentional. Encounter is the regime where cycles meet, and the meeting is shaped by the grid that contains it.
