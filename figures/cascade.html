The cycle, ageing.

One maintenance cycle of the immune system, sixteen positions, run from age 20 to 90. The cycle does not fail all at once. It fails in an order: the build of the new goes first, the correcting arm exhausts, the patrol runs hot, and the closure that lets the cycle compound its own work goes last. Move age forward and watch the order arrive.

How to read it

The ring is one cycle, sixteen positions, four regimes: Potentiality top-left, Construction top-right, Encounter bottom-right, Conservation bottom-left. Traversal is clockwise. Dot brightness is current stock at that position.

Two positions inside Conservation carry the maintenance work this page is about. P14 is patrolling, the watch the system rebuilds and runs over itself. P15 is correcting, the small engagement that resolves what the watch flags. The arc from P16 back to P1 is closure: the handoff where a finished round becomes the substrate the next round loads. Closure is where the cycle compounds its own investment. When it carries less than a full unit, the difference is lost as deficit and the cycle stops building on itself.

The order is the claim

Under a contracting budget the most forward-looking work is cut first. The build of new capacity is the surplus the cycle produces beyond keeping itself alive, so reproduction fails first: thinner thymic output, fewer naive cells, less repertoire built. The correcting arm then runs against more than it can clear and exhausts second. The patrol, receiving an unreset, under-maintained field, runs hot third, the inflammation that is usually read as a cause rather than a third-stage consequence. Closure carries less and less, so integration fails last: the cycle can no longer compound, which is what failure to form new durable protection looks like.

The order survives wide variation in the rates. The absolute timing does not. What this page argues is the sequence, not the calendar.

Model & parameters

Discrete-time stocks-and-flows over sixteen positions. A constant low input enters at P1 (signal is always present). Stock advances one position per step. Construction (P5–P8) applies a production gain with logistic self-limiting, this is the build of new capacity. P15 throughput is capped by a correcting-capacity term. P14 clears only a fraction of what arrives; the remainder accumulates and the dot runs hot. The P16→P1 handoff carries a fraction R of its outflow; (1−R) is logged as closure deficit ρ. Four age-driven curves degrade in staggered order.

Reproduction gain (P5–P8)fails firstlogistic centre ≈ age 40
Correcting capacity (P15)fails secondlogistic centre ≈ age 57
Patrol clearance (P14)fails thirdlogistic centre ≈ age 67
Closure carried fraction R (P16→P1)fails lastlogistic centre ≈ age 78

All constants are tuned, not derived from clinical data. They are chosen so the structural argument is visible. The qualitative ordering of the four failures holds across wide variation in every constant; the absolute magnitudes and the ages do not. This is a working hypothesis with disclosed parameters, not a clinical model. Age milestones are illustrative and loosely echo thymic involution (early), inflammaging (mid–late), and vaccine hyporesponse (late).
